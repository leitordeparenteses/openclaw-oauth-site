OpenClaw – Personal Google Workspace Assistant

This is a private, personal-use OpenClaw integration used to let an AI assistant work with Google Workspace services on behalf of its authorized user.

What the app does

The integration can access Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Gmail and Calendar when the authorized user explicitly asks the assistant to perform a task, such as listing files, reading or creating documents, working with spreadsheets, sending email, or managing calendar events.

Who can use it

This app is intended for personal use by its owner and specifically authorized accounts. It is not offered as a public SaaS product and does not accept public sign-ups.

Data handling

Google account data is accessed only to fulfill user-requested actions. OAuth credentials are stored locally in the user's OpenClaw environment. When required to complete a request, relevant content may be processed by AI model providers configured by the user in OpenClaw.
